# About Abdulrahman Hafez — ARHAFEZ

Canonical: https://arhafez.com/about

I'm Abdulrahman Hafez, a strategic brand designer. I've built brands across Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and the UAE — from banking to real estate to film and production.

## ARHAFEZ is my name.

I put it on every project because I stand behind it, personally. You're not hiring an anonymous studio — you're working with the person responsible for the result.

## Design is a business tool, not decoration.

A logo is the easy part. What matters is the system underneath it — the strategy, the structure, the decisions that keep a brand clear as it grows. That's the work I care about, and it's why every project starts with strategy.

## Where it's going.

ARHAFEZ started as my own name, and it's growing into something larger — on purpose. Same name, same belief, from the first project to wherever this goes.
